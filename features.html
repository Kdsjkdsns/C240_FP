Product tools

Use VeriFox to understand scam risk in seconds.

VeriFox lets you ask general scam-related questions, paste suspicious messages or links, or upload screenshots of suspicious messages or websites to receive a scam risk analysis.

1. Scam Q&A Chatbot

Ask VeriFox general questions about scams in Singapore, such as “What is phishing?” or “Can police officers ask me to transfer money?” The chatbot answers using Singapore scam safety knowledge.

Suggested answer: “Treat this as suspicious until verified. Do not click links or share OTPs.”

2. Suspicious Message and Link Checker

Paste a suspicious message or link. VeriFox returns a risk score, risk level, possible scam type, summary, red flags found, recommended actions, and a Singapore scam guidance reference.

Risk score: 84/100 — urgent caution and verify through official channels.

3. Screenshot Checker

Upload a screenshot of a suspicious message, website, email, or chat — for example an SMS scam, WhatsApp scam, Telegram message, or fake bank login page. VeriFox extracts the visible text and analyses it for scam risk.

Detected signs: pressure tactics, urgency, requests for personal details.

4. Risk Score and Risk Level

VeriFox gives a score from 0 to 100 so you can quickly understand the danger level.

Score Risk Level Meaning
0–25 Low Few scam signs found
26–50 Medium Some suspicious signs found
51–75 High Many scam signs found
76–100 Critical Strong scam signs or immediate danger

5. Scam Warning Sign Explanation

VeriFox explains why something may be suspicious, instead of giving a plain yes/no answer.

“This message appears suspicious because it uses urgent language, claims your account is locked, and asks you to click an unknown link. These are common warning signs of phishing scams.”

6. Recommended Safety Actions

Clear next steps, such as:

  • Do not click the link
  • Do not reply to the sender
  • Do not share OTPs, passwords, Singpass details, or bank details
  • Do not transfer money
  • Verify through official websites, official apps, or official hotlines
  • Contact the bank immediately if sensitive information was shared
  • Make a police report if money was lost

7. Two-Agent Verification

VeriFox uses two AI agents for safer results.

Agent 1: Scam Risk Analyser

Checks the submitted message, link, or screenshot and identifies scam signs.

Agent 2: Safety Verifier

Reviews Agent 1's result before showing it to the user. It checks whether the risk score, explanation, and advice are reasonable and safe.

8. Singapore Scam Knowledge Base

VeriFox uses Singapore scam-safety knowledge through retrieval-augmented generation (RAG). The knowledge base includes information about:

  • Phishing scams
  • Bank impersonation scams
  • Government or police impersonation scams
  • Delivery scams
  • Job scams
  • OTP and password safety
  • Suspicious links
  • What to do after sharing sensitive information
  • What to do after transferring money
  • ScamShield and Singapore scam safety guidance

9. PDF Report Generation

After a scam check is completed, generate a downloadable report with the risk score, risk level, possible scam type, summary, red flags found, recommended actions, Singapore scam guidance used, verification note, and disclaimer.

PDF export ready for chat, family, or evidence sharing.

Note: this feature is PDF report generation only. The current version does not allow users to upload PDFs for scam checking.
