Problem and purpose

Why VeriFox exists

Many people in Singapore receive suspicious messages, calls and documents that look real. VeriFox gives users a fast way to check whether content is likely a scam and what to do next.

The problem

Scammers impersonate banks, government agencies, police, delivery services and recruiters. Users can be pressured to share OTPs, bank details, passwords or transfer money before they verify the message.

The need

People want a simple assistant that explains whether content is suspicious, why it looks suspicious, and how to stay safe without jargon or overconfidence.

VeriFox solution

  • Ask scam questions backed by Singapore fraud guidance
  • Check suspicious text, links, screenshots, documents and videos
  • Receive a scam risk score, explanation, and recommended actions
  • Use voice input when typing is inconvenient
  • Download a report for relatives or evidence

Clear guidance

VeriFox focuses on warning signs and safety actions, not just a binary yes/no answer.

Singapore aware

Advice reflects local scam behaviours, reporting channels, and examples that resonate with users in Singapore.

Trusted tone

The assistant is calm, practical and designed to reduce fear while staying truthful about risk.
