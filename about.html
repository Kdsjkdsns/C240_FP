Problem and purpose

Why VeriFox exists

People in Singapore often receive suspicious messages, links, screenshots, emails, and online scam attempts. These scams may pretend to be from banks, government agencies, delivery services, job recruiters, or police officers.

The problem

Many users may not know whether a message is safe or dangerous. This can lead to users clicking phishing links, sharing OTPs or passwords, giving bank details, installing unsafe apps, or transferring money to scammers.

The need

People want a simple assistant that explains whether content is suspicious, why it looks suspicious, and how to stay safe without jargon or overconfidence.

VeriFox solution

VeriFox gives users a simple way to check suspicious content and receive clear, Singapore-focused scam safety guidance.

  • Ask scam questions backed by Singapore scam-safety knowledge
  • Check suspicious messages, links, and screenshots
  • Receive a scam risk score, explanation, and recommended actions
  • Get results reviewed by a second AI safety checker
  • Download a PDF report for records or evidence
